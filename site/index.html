Motion

Watch at your screen's full frame rate.

Films and most online video are made at 24 or 25 frames a second, so pans judder on a 60, 120 or 144 Hz screen. NOVA Motion makes the frames in between with AI, live, while you watch. The frames from the video itself are never touched.

  • Fits your graphics card. A one-time tuning run measures each AI model on your PC, and the engine uses the best one that keeps up. On cards like a GTX 1650 the AI works on a half-size copy, four times less work.
  • YouTube and 1,000+ sites. Paste a link, or send the video you're watching from Chrome or Edge with the browser extension. It picks up where you stopped.
  • Steady, never stuck. If frames start dropping it steps to a lighter setting by itself instead of stuttering. One click plays without smoothing.
  • A player like the best ones. Seek previews, Skip intro, picture in picture, subtitles, night mode for quieter explosions and clearer voices, live sharpening and Anime4K.

Enhance videos

Cleaner, sharper, smoother copies.

Pick a preset and see how long it will take on your PC before anything starts. AI cleans and enlarges every frame up to 8K, RIFE makes it 60 fps, and the copy plays smoothly anywhere: a phone, a TV, an upload.

  • Presets for real videos. Anime episode, Film & live action, Old or low-res, Blocky download, 4K remaster, Just smooth. Every choice is one tap away in Customize.
  • See it first. Preview one full-size before/after frame in seconds, or 4 seconds of video, before you spend an hour exporting.
  • The right model for the picture. Real-ESRGAN for film and live action, Real-CUGAN for anime and art, DPIR to fix compression blocks.
  • Before/after clips to share. A labelled side-by-side or split, made for posting.

Enhance photos

More detail than the photo had.

One picture can take the heaviest models a whole video can't. Enlarge to print, fix pictures saved from chats, remove blur, or let generative AI draw new detail that fits.

Generative detail New

  • It looks at the whole picture. SeedVR2, a diffusion model, makes eyelashes, fur, skin texture and the crumb of bread where a phone smeared them, instead of only sharpening what's there. No prompt: the photo is its context.
  • Only where you want it. Drag over a face or a pet to generate just that part, blended into the rest. Strength mixes in the original for a natural look.
  • Honest about time. On a GTX 1650 (4 GB) about 90 seconds a megapixel: a face in about a minute, a 12 MP photo about 20 minutes. Much faster on RTX cards. Needs an NVIDIA card with 4 GB or more.
  • Remove noise and blur (NAFNet) and Max detail (Real-ESRGAN) run on any card, in seconds.

Generate

Bring a still picture to life.

Animate a picture turns a still into a short 3D camera move: push in, drift, rise, sway or orbit. AI works out what is near and far, and near things move more. Made for illustration, anime and posters, and it runs on any graphics card, laptop graphics included.

  • Straight into the rest of the studio. Clips land in Videos\NOVA Studio, ready to smooth to 60 fps or enlarge in Enhance.
  • Coming next: images and short clips from open models (Flux, SDXL, Wan 2.2). The page already checks what your PC can run.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. NOVA Studio is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0, made by Novabit. There's no account, no subscription and no watermark, and the AI models it uses all allow commercial use.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Every model runs on your own graphics card. The only things NOVA Studio downloads are its parts (from the AI Models page) and the online videos you ask it to play.

Will it work on my laptop?

Very likely. It's built and tested on a GTX 1650 laptop with 4 GB. Motion measures your card once and picks what keeps up, and every feature says up front whether this PC can run it.

How is generative detail different from sharpening?

Sharpening and classic upscaling can only make existing edges crisper. Generative detail draws new detail that fits the scene, the way a painter would, which is why it can bring back texture a phone's processing smeared away. It's slower, so you can apply it to just a part of the photo.

Does it replace SVP or Topaz?

It covers the same ground, live motion smoothing and AI enhancement, as one free, open app. It's an independent project that shares no code with either.
