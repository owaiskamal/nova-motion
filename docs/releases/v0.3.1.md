**Better anime, a cleaner picture, and fixes.** Everything here was tested on a GTX 1650 laptop and an RTX 4070 desktop.

## Install

1. Download **NovaMotion-Setup-0.3.1** below and run it. It updates 0.3.0 in place and keeps your settings and AI models.
2. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

**Anime**
- **Keep drawings as drawn** (on in the Anime episode preset; also in Motion settings and on the Export page): anime holds each drawing for 2 or 3 frames, and smoothing between two drawings morphs them (melting faces). Now held drawings and the switch between them are shown as drawn, and only camera pans and moves are smoothed. It also uses less of the graphics card.
- **No more waxy anime at 4K:** big enlargements (such as 480p to 4K) now use Real-CUGAN, which keeps line art and shading, instead of a model that flattened them.
- The Anime episode preset adds **fine grain** so cleaned flat areas don't look plastic.

**Enhance**
- **Colour stays true:** the AI models drifted colour and brightness slightly; the result now keeps the original's. Measured against the true pictures, the drift fell from about 1.4 levels of 255 to 0.1.
- **Grain you can see**, strongest in the mid-tones and fading towards black and white, as film grain does. It now also works on PCs without TensorRT.
- **Before/after: Split, Side by side or 100% detail.** The old view was always zoomed in.

**Fixes**
- Exporting a part far into a long video (such as 9 seconds from 4:18 of a film) failed with "The AI engine didn't run".
- A finished export could be deleted and shown as "Cancelled".
- Photos named in Urdu, Arabic, Chinese or with an emoji failed at the last step.
- Engine scripts could stay at an old version after an update (Enhance kept showing "Needs preparing").
- Quitting could leave background work running; the app now says what's running and stops it.
- An error could leave an export or download stuck as busy.
- Side-by-side before/after exports wider than 4K failed on NVIDIA's encoder.
- Installing Enhance when GitHub was limiting downloads said "the models aren't published yet"; it now says to try again in a few minutes.
