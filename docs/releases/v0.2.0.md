**Watch at your screen's full frame rate.** NOVA Motion makes 24 and 25 fps video play smoothly by generating the frames in between with AI, live, on your own graphics card. Free and open source for Windows.

## Install

1. Download **NovaMotion-Setup** below and run it. No admin rights needed.
2. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. On NVIDIA RTX or GTX 16 cards, run **Add NVIDIA TensorRT engine** from the Start menu once (about 3 GB). It's the fastest engine and it tunes itself to your card.

Building from source instead? Run `build.cmd`; it sets everything up. See the [README](https://github.com/owaiskamal/nova-motion#get-started).

## What's in this release

- **Real-time AI smoothing** with RIFE on NVIDIA TensorRT, RIFE on Vulkan (any modern GPU) or MVTools, for local files, YouTube and 1,000+ sites.
- **Fits your graphics card:** tuning measures each model on your PC. On cards like a GTX 1650 the AI works on a half-size copy and only its frames are scaled up; real frames stay untouched.
- **Even frame pacing** on 120 and 144 Hz screens, and frame rates the AI can actually keep up with.
- **Steady, never stuck:** steps to a lighter setting instead of stuttering, and **Play without smoothing** is always one click away.
- **Enhance:** smooth copies of files, and labelled before/after clips to share.
- A full player: resume, folder queue, subtitles, audio tracks, online quality, a browser extension for Chrome and Edge.

## Tested on

| PC | Result |
| --- | --- |
| RTX 4070 desktop | 24 fps film to 60, smooth, AI at full size |
| GTX 1650 laptop, 144 Hz | 24 fps film to 72, smooth, AI at half size |

Intel and AMD graphics are supported through Vulkan and MVTools but tested less so far. If something isn't smooth, run `collect-logs.cmd` (in the install folder) and attach the zip to an [issue](https://github.com/owaiskamal/nova-motion/issues).
