**AI for your videos and photos, on your own PC.** NOVA Motion is now **NOVA Studio**: live motion smoothing, AI enhancement for videos and photos, generative detail, and Animate a picture, in one free, open-source app for Windows.

## Install

1. Download **NovaMotion-Setup-0.3.0** below and run it. No admin rights needed. It updates 0.2.0 in place and keeps your settings.
2. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open **AI Models** in the sidebar and download what you want. Each part shows its size for your graphics card, real progress, and pauses and resumes. Every feature also offers its own part the first time you use it.

## What's new since 0.2.0

**Enhance videos**
- AI clean-up and upscaling to 1080p, 1440p, 4K and 8K (Real-ESRGAN for film, Real-CUGAN for anime, DPIR to fix compression), mixed with the original by AI strength so real footage doesn't look painted.
- Presets (Anime episode, Film & live action, Old or low-res, Blocky download, 4K remaster, Just smooth) and **the time it will take on your PC before anything starts**.
- **Preview a frame** in seconds or **Preview 4 s**, as a before/after, before a long export.

**Enhance photos (new)**
- Presets for phone photos, prints, chats and art; same size, 2× or 4×, up to 8K.
- **Max detail** (Real-ESRGAN x4plus), **Remove noise and blur** (NAFNet) and **Fix compression**.
- **Generative detail** (SeedVR2 3B): draws new detail that fits the picture, such as fur, eyelashes and texture. Drag over a face or a pet to do just that part. NVIDIA cards with 4 GB or more; on a GTX 1650 about 90 seconds a megapixel.
- A 100% before/after of any spot.

**Generate (new)**
- **Animate a picture:** a still becomes a short 3D camera move (push in, drift, rise, sway, orbit). Any graphics card.

**Setup inside the app (new)**
- The **AI Models** page installs everything with no console windows: real progress, pause and resume, checksums, a disk-space check first, and models prepared for your graphics card right after they download.
- **Save diagnostics** in Settings makes one zip of every log to attach to a problem report.

**Motion**
- A new player: seek previews, Skip intro and recap, picture in picture, night mode, an A–B loop, sleep timer and more.
- Live **Crisp**, **Extra crisp** and **Anime4K** on any graphics card.
- 4K video is smoothed at 1080p and enlarged by the player, so it no longer lags on 4K screens; a video above 1080p that drops frames goes to 1080p before a lighter model.
- A video size the AI hasn't seen plays at once while its engine is prepared in the background.

**Design**
- A new sidebar design (collapse it with Ctrl+B), presets everywhere, and an **Activity** panel that shows every export and model preparation with the time left.

## Tested on

| PC | Result |
| --- | --- |
| RTX 4070 desktop | 24 fps film to 60, smooth at full size; Generative detail with the 8-bit model |
| GTX 1650 laptop (4 GB), 144 Hz | 24 fps film to 72, smooth with the AI at half size; Generative detail with the 4-bit model |

Intel and AMD graphics are supported through Vulkan and MVTools but tested less. If something isn't right, use **Settings → Save diagnostics** and attach the zip to an [issue](https://github.com/owaiskamal/nova-motion/issues).
